About

profile

I came up through the SOC — analyst at TCS, SOC Lead at Deloitte’s Cyber Risk Fusion Center, malware and threat analyst — and now lead cloud security and security operations for a PCI DSS-compliant cloud payment platform at Compest Solutions.

Over 9+ years the work has shifted from triaging alerts to building the functions that handle them: detection and response stood up from scratch, SOAR automation that cut MTTR ~50%, and the identity, secrets, and certificate governance that keeps a payment platform audit-ready — two consecutive PCI DSS QSA attestations. With a CISM and an M.S. in Cybersecurity, I run SecOps as a business-aligned program: measured in KPIs, reported to leadership, and built to scale.

I build security programs from nothing rather than inheriting them — a PCI DSS cloud payment environment stood up end to end, from architecture and control design through assessment readiness. And I keep the hands-on credibility of a decade in SOC, threat hunting, malware analysis, and forensics work, which is what lets me lead a team on technical judgment rather than process alone.
